# iStrongEngine Privacy Notice

Version 1.0, 9 November 2026

This notice says what information iStrongEngine and its official services keep about you, why, for how long, and who can see it. It is written by the creator of iStrongEngine, Arjuna (the creator). To ask anything about it, or to ask for your information, write to the creator on the official Discord.

## 1. The app keeps nothing about you

iStrongEngine runs in your browser, on your own computer. Your projects, settings, designs and files stay in your browser's storage or in the files you save. They are never sent to the creator or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no ads, no cookies, and no tracking or analytics. Your I Agree answer is kept in your browser only.

To load its fonts, the app asks Google Fonts for them. Like any website, Google can see your IP address and your browser when it sends them. Google's own privacy policy covers that.

## 2. The beta site

The beta site lets testers use iStrongEngine before it is public. When you enter an access token there, the site records:

- your IP address, your browser, your system and your kind of device;
- the date and time, the token you used, and whether it worked;
- for an early access token: your internet provider, its network number and your country, the first time you use it.

Why: to keep each token to the person it was given to, to lock it to the connection it was first used from, and to stop sharing, attacks and abuse. Cloudflare's own check against bots (Turnstile) may run on the page.

How long: the login records are deleted after 90 days. A token and its lock stay while the token exists, and end when it is taken away or when you ask.

To check a suspected misuse, the creator may look up the rough place of an IP address with an outside lookup service (ipapi.is or ipwho.is), which then receives that IP address.

## 3. The Discord server and its bot

If you join the official Discord server, its bot records your Discord user ID and name, when your Discord account was made, when you joined and left the server, which invite link you came through and who made it, and whether you hold the Early Access role. If it gives you an early access token, it keeps that token with your Discord ID and name, and its use as in section 2.

Why: to run the invite promotion and to give out early access fairly.

How long: the records of token use are deleted after 90 days, and the bot's log of its own actions after one year. The rest stays while the promotion and early access run, and is removed when you ask.

Discord itself is covered by Discord's own privacy policy.

## 4. Hosting

The beta site and the bot run on Cloudflare, which keeps its own technical records and visitor statistics for the services it runs. Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers those.

## 5. Who can see it

Only the creator. Moderators the creator chooses can see testers' names and how often they used the beta, never their IP addresses. Nothing is sold, rented, used for ads, or given to anyone else, except to the services named in this notice, for the reasons given here.

## 6. Your choices

You can ask what is kept about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to the creator on the official Discord. Deleting an early access token ends that early access. The laws of some places give you more rights; ask, and they will be honoured.

## 7. Age

The app itself keeps nothing about anyone, so anyone may use it. The beta site and the Discord server are for people aged 13 or older, or the older age Discord asks for in your country. If you believe a younger child gave information to them, tell the creator and it will be deleted.

## 8. Changes

This notice can change. A new version is shown in the app with its version and date.
